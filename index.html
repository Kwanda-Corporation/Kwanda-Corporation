Kwanda Corporations
Where software meets art.

Kwanda Corporations — also known as Kwanda, Kwanda Corporation or Kwanda Group — is a Rwanda-origin technology company focused on designing, developing and building digital products, software systems and technology-driven solutions that address real-world challenges. Its work brings software engineering, creativity, innovation and entrepreneurship together to transform ideas into practical products.

About Kwanda Corporations

Founded on the belief that technology should serve people and solve meaningful problems, Kwanda Corporations is a product-oriented company: it identifies problems, develops solutions, launches products, learns from real users and continuously improves the systems it builds. The company is registered in Kigali, Rwanda, and aims to build technology that can grow beyond its initial market, serve communities and contribute to the digital transformation of Africa.

The name Kwanda comes from the Kinyarwanda word for expanding, growing and increasing. That meaning reflects the company's philosophy: growth should not be limited to the company itself. Technology should expand opportunities, improve access to services, connect people and create possibilities that did not previously exist.

Products & projects

Katisha — East African travel booking

Katisha is a digital travel booking platform originating in Rwanda, designed to make travelling across East Africa easier, more accessible and more convenient — without a trip to the bus station. It connects passengers with transportation operators and brings digital discovery, online booking, operator integration and digital payments into one platform.

Katisha is a live product. Bookings happen at katisha.today.

STRS — Student Transport and Routing System

STRS is a proposed system designed to connect the people and organisations responsible for getting students to school: schools, parents, transport operators and education authorities. It aims to make student transport organised, visible and accounted for, and extends Kwanda's work from commercial travel into education-related digital infrastructure.

How Kwanda works

Kwanda follows a product-oriented approach: Discover problems and opportunities, Imagine creative approaches, Design meaningful experiences and system architectures, Engineer reliable and scalable software, Launch to real users, and Evolve through usage, feedback and changing needs.

  • Engineering — turn ideas into working solutions.
  • Purpose — build technology that addresses meaningful problems.
  • Growth — keep learning, improving and expanding.
  • Collaboration — create better outcomes with different minds.
