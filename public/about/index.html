About · Kigali, Rwanda

Software built with purpose and craft.

Kwanda Corporations is a Rwanda-origin technology company that designs, develops and builds digital products and software systems. We turn real-world problems into working products — then stay with them through launch, use and continuous improvement.

Our story

Kwanda Corporations Ltd is registered and based in Kigali, Rwanda. The company was founded on a simple conviction: technology should serve people and solve meaningful problems. We are a product-oriented company — we identify problems, develop solutions, launch products, learn from real users and keep improving the systems we build.

Our work spans transportation technology, education technology, digital payments and cloud infrastructure. Our first live product, Katisha, connects passengers with bus operators across Rwanda and East Africa. Our second initiative, Gerayo, applies the same product discipline to student transport and routing.

What the name means

Kwanda comes from the Kinyarwanda word for expanding, growing and increasing. That meaning shapes how we work: growth should not stop at the company. Technology should expand opportunities, improve access to services, connect people and create possibilities that did not previously exist.

What we do

Product 01 · Live

Katisha

An online bus ticket booking platform for Rwanda and East Africa. Search routes, compare journeys, book a seat and travel with a digital ticket.

Product 02 · Proposed

Gerayo

A connected way for schools, parents, operators and education authorities to organise student journeys.

How we work

We follow a product-oriented approach: Discover problems and opportunities, Imagine creative approaches, Design meaningful experiences and system architectures, Engineer reliable and scalable software, Launch to real users, and Evolve through usage, feedback and changing needs.

  • Creativity — ask what is possible before what is practical.
  • Engineering — turn ideas into working solutions.
  • Purpose — build technology that addresses meaningful problems.
  • Growth — keep learning, improving and expanding.
  • Collaboration — create better outcomes with different minds.
