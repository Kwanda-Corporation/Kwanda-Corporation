Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions.

Clear answers about Kwanda Corporations, our products and how the company relates to other organisations that share the Kwanda name.

Company

What is Kwanda Corporations?

Kwanda Corporations is a Rwanda-origin technology company focused on designing, developing and building digital products, software systems and technology-driven solutions that address real-world challenges. Its work spans transportation technology, education technology, digital payments and cloud infrastructure.

Where is Kwanda Corporations based?

Kwanda Corporations is registered and based in Kigali, Rwanda, and builds technology intended to serve Rwanda, East Africa and the wider African market.

Is Kwanda Corporations the same as Kwanda Group?

No. Kwanda Corporations Ltd is a distinct software and technology company registered in Kigali, Rwanda. Kwanda Group is a separate organisation and is not an alias, parent, subsidiary or affiliate of Kwanda Corporations. Kwanda Corporations is also not affiliated with Kwanda Ltd (UK), Kwanda Technology PTY, Vision Kwanda Group or Kwanda Industrial.

What does the name Kwanda mean?

Kwanda comes from the Kinyarwanda word for expanding, growing and increasing. It reflects the company's philosophy that technology should expand opportunities, improve access to services and connect people.

Who leads Kwanda Corporations?

Kwanda Corporations is led by founder and Chief Executive Officer Manzi Alpe, with Ashraf Tuyubahe as Chief Technology Officer, Ntwali Josue as Lead Software Engineer and Gatare Jean Marie as Operations Manager. Meet the team →

Products

What products does Kwanda Corporations build?

Kwanda Corporations builds Katisha, a live digital travel booking platform for East Africa, and Gerayo, our proposed student transport and routing system for schools, parents, operators and education authorities.

What is Katisha?

Katisha is an online bus ticket booking platform for Rwanda and East Africa. It brings digital discovery, online booking, operator integration and digital payments into one platform so passengers can plan and book bus journeys without visiting a station. Katisha is live at katisha.today.

How do I book a bus ticket with Katisha?

Open katisha.today, enter your route and travel date, compare the journeys available, choose a seat and complete the booking with a supported payment method. You then receive a digital QR ticket on your phone.

Can I pay for Katisha tickets with mobile money?

Yes. Katisha supports mobile money payments, including MTN MoMo and Airtel Money, and issues a digital QR ticket for boarding.

How do I contact Katisha support?

Email support@katisha.today or call +250 793 216 191.

What is Gerayo?

Gerayo is a proposed student transport and routing system designed to give schools, parents, transport operators and education authorities a connected way to organise, route and account for student journeys.

Is Gerayo available today?

No. Gerayo is currently a proposed system in the product-development stage. Katisha is Kwanda Corporations' live product.
