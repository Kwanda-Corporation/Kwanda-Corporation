Katisha: bus tickets, without the queue.

Katisha is an online bus booking platform for Rwanda and East Africa. Search a route, compare the journeys available, book a seat and travel with a digital ticket — from your phone, before you reach the station.

Book on katisha.today How it works

What is Katisha?

Katisha is a digital travel booking platform that originated in Rwanda. It brings route discovery, online booking, operator integration and digital payments into one experience, so passengers can plan and book intercity and cross-border bus journeys across East Africa without visiting a ticket office.

The platform is live at katisha.today. It is a product of Kwanda Corporations Ltd, a software and technology company based in Kigali.

How Katisha works

Step 01

Search

Enter your route and travel date to see the journeys available, in one place.

Step 02

Compare

Review departure times, routes, operators and options side by side.

Step 03

Book

Choose a seat, confirm the booking and pay digitally with mobile money.

Step 04

Travel

Board with your digital QR ticket on your phone — no printed ticket needed.

Who Katisha is for

  • Passengers in Rwanda booking intercity journeys from Kigali and other cities.
  • Cross-border travellers planning journeys between Rwanda and neighbouring East African countries.
  • People booking from another location — arranging a journey for yourself, family or a colleague.
  • Bus operators who want their schedules discoverable and their seats bookable online.

What you can do with Katisha

  • Discover available bus journeys by route and date.
  • Compare departure times, routes and operators in one place.
  • Book a seat online and receive a digital confirmation.
  • Pay with mobile money, including MTN MoMo and Airtel Money.
  • Board using a QR ticket on your phone.
  • Arrange travel without queueing at a station ticket desk.

Rwanda and East Africa coverage

Katisha is built around Rwandan travel and the wider East African region. Its focus is practical coverage: intercity routes from Kigali, cross-border journeys, and a growing network of transport operators. Live route availability and operators are always shown when you search on katisha.today.

Katisha works with transport operators in the region, including Trinity Express and Simba Coaches. The operator running your journey is shown during booking.

Why travellers use Katisha

Bus travel in East Africa is often organised through physical stations, informal information and last-minute queues. Katisha replaces that friction with a simple digital flow: know what is available, choose with confidence, and carry your ticket on your phone.

Katisha is a booking platform, not a bus company. It connects passengers with transport operators. The company that operates your bus is identified during booking.

Katisha FAQ

What is Katisha?

Katisha is an online bus ticket booking platform for Rwanda and East Africa. It lets travellers search routes, compare journeys, book a seat and travel with a digital ticket, without first visiting a bus station.

How do I book a bus ticket in Rwanda with Katisha?

Open katisha.today, enter your route and travel date, compare the available journeys, choose a seat, and complete the booking with a supported payment method. Your confirmation and digital ticket are then available on your phone.

Which routes and countries does Katisha cover?

Katisha focuses on Rwanda and East Africa, covering intercity journeys from Kigali and cross-border travel in the region. Available routes and operators are shown when you search on katisha.today.

Can I pay for Katisha tickets with mobile money?

Yes. Katisha supports mobile money payments, including MTN MoMo and Airtel Money, and issues a digital QR ticket for boarding.

Is Katisha a bus company?

No. Katisha is a booking platform, not a bus operator. It connects passengers with transport operators. The operator running your journey is shown during booking.
