STRS: a connected approach to student transport.

STRS — the Student Transport and Routing System — is a proposed platform that connects the people and organisations responsible for getting students to school: schools, parents, transport operators and education authorities.

Discuss STRS See our live product

The problem STRS addresses

Student transport is a daily coordination challenge. Schools need to know who is expected, parents need confidence that their child is on the right vehicle, operators need clear routes and passenger lists, and authorities need oversight of safety and accountability.

In practice, that information is often spread across phone calls, paper lists and disconnected conversations. STRS is designed to bring it into one shared system.

Who STRS connects

Schools

Plan and account

Organise which students travel, on which routes, and reconcile daily attendance and transport records.

Parents & guardians

Know the journey

Understand the route, schedule and arrangements for a child's daily trip to and from school.

Transport operators

Run clearer routes

Work from defined routes, stops and passenger lists instead of ad-hoc coordination.

Education authorities

See the system

Gain visibility into student transport arrangements, coverage and accountability across schools.

What STRS is designed to do

  • Define student transport routes, stops and schedules.
  • Connect students and guardians to the right route and vehicle.
  • Give schools a shared view of who is travelling and when.
  • Support transport operators with organised passenger information.
  • Create an accountable record of student journeys.
  • Give education authorities visibility across the system.

Status: proposed system. STRS is in the product-development stage and is not yet available for use. This page describes the problem and the intended design, not a live service.

Why Kwanda is building STRS

STRS extends the work Kwanda already does in transport technology. Our live product, Katisha, connects passengers with bus operators across Rwanda and East Africa. STRS applies similar principles — discovery, coordination, visibility and accountability — to the daily journeys that families depend on most.

It is part of a wider goal: build digital infrastructure that makes everyday services easier to reach and easier to trust.

STRS FAQ

What is STRS?

STRS stands for Student Transport and Routing System. It is a proposed system designed to connect schools, parents, transport operators and education authorities so student journeys can be organised, routed and accounted for.

Is STRS available today?

No. STRS is currently a proposed system in the product-development stage. Katisha, Kwanda Corporations' travel booking platform, is the company's live product.

Who is STRS for?

STRS is designed for schools, parents and guardians, student transport operators, and education authorities.

Who builds STRS?

STRS is developed by Kwanda Corporations Ltd, a software and technology company based in Kigali, Rwanda.
