How the team works

Kwanda operates as a product team rather than a traditional agency. Strategy, design and engineering sit close together, which keeps decisions short and feedback fast. The team follows a repeatable arc: Discover, Imagine, Design, Engineer, Launch and Evolve.

That approach produced Katisha, a live bus booking platform for Rwanda and East Africa, and is now being applied to Gerayo.
